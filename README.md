A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it. `main.ts` (Node 22, no network, deterministic) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`.

The text is a quiet scene after the last verdict: the page going dark on its own, the cursor stopping, a half-full mug still warm enough to hold, and words settling like dust in late light. It shows calm through concrete images rather than instructing the reader to relax.
